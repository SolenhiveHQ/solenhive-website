Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Marc Carlton LLC, doing business as Solenhive ("Solenhive," "we," "us," or "our"), operates the website at solenhive.com (the "Site"). This policy explains what information we collect when you visit the Site or contact us, how we use it, who receives it, and the choices you have.

This policy covers the Site and inquiries you send us. Data we process for clients through our software platform or under a consulting engagement is governed by the written agreement with that client.

Information We Collect

Information you send us. The contact form on the Site opens your own email app with your details filled in. We receive only what you choose to send: typically your name, work email, company, how many locations or clients you have, and your message. The same applies when you email us directly.

Information collected automatically. When your browser loads the Site, it sends standard technical information: your IP address, browser and device type, the pages requested, the referring page, and the time of the visit. Our hosting provider records this to deliver and protect the Site.

Cookies. We do not set our own cookies. The Amazon Ads tag described below may place and read cookies.

How We Use Information

  • To reply to your inquiry and provide the services you ask about
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site
  • To measure our advertising: how many people visit and how many contact us
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and Cookies

We use the Amazon Ads tag to measure our advertising. It records a page view when a page loads and a contact event when you click our email address or send the contact form. To do this, Amazon may place and read cookies and collect your IP address and browser and device information.

Third parties, including Amazon, may place and use cookies on the Site to show you interest-based ads on other websites and apps based on your visits here.

You can opt out of interest-based ads from Amazon on Amazon's Advertising Preferences page, and from participating companies through the Digital Advertising Alliance, Your Online Choices (Europe), or the Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Privacy signals. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, the Site does not load the Amazon Ads tag.

Who Receives Information

We share information only with the service providers that run the Site and our business, and only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Vercel hosts the Site and keeps request logs.
  • Google Fonts serves the Site's typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google's servers, which shares your IP address and browser information with Google.
  • Amazon Ads measures our advertising, as described above.
  • Google Workspace hosts our email, including messages you send to hello@solenhive.com.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the rights, property, or safety of Solenhive, our clients, or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case the recipient must honor this policy.

Data Retention

We keep inquiry emails for as long as we need them to respond and to maintain our business records, and we delete them on request unless the law requires us to keep them. Hosting logs and advertising data are kept by those providers under their own retention schedules.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and receive a copy
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Object to or restrict how we use it
  • Opt out of targeted advertising, as described above

To make a request, email hello@solenhive.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law, and we will not treat you differently for exercising these rights.

Children's Privacy

The Site is a business website and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

International Visitors

The Site is operated from the United States, and the information described here is processed in the United States.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
