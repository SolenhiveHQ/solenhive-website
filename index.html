What broken tracking costs.

Bad tags don't just break reports. They feed the ad platforms the wrong signals, and the media budget pays for it. These four come from the scan above.

  1. Error

    No lead events reach Meta.

    Four of six Meta pixels send nothing; the other two send only PageView. Campaigns optimize without knowing who became a lead.

  2. Error

    Google Ads can't see conversions.

    Six of seven conversion tags never fire, so automated bidding works without the signal it bids on.

  3. Warning

    Analytics is split twelve ways.

    Twelve GA4 properties fire at once from overlapping scripts, so no single property holds the full picture.

  4. Warning

    Tags load more than once.

    Seven tag containers load multiple times per page, wasting requests and risking double-counted conversions.
